SECTION 05: MY BUSINESS PHILOSOPHY
WHAT I BELIEVE ABOUT BUILDING A BLOGGING BUSINESS
A few things I believe that might challenge everything you've been taught about blogging.
01. You don't need an enormous audience to begin making money.
You need the right people, a meaningful problem to solve and an offer those people have a reason to purchase.
Growing your audience can support your business, but audience size alone doesn't determine whether you have a commercially valuable offer.
02. Your business strategy should come before your website.
Before we decide what your website looks like, we need to understand who you're serving, what you're selling and what your website needs to accomplish.
Design becomes far more powerful when there's a business strategy behind it.
03. Understanding people is one of the most valuable business skills you can develop.
Buyer psychology influences your offers, messaging, marketing, sales and customer experience.
The better you understand the people you're serving, the more informed your business decisions become.
04. You don't have to monetize the same way everyone else does.
Advertising and affiliate marketing are possibilities, not prerequisites.
Your knowledge, expertise, services, frameworks and ability to help people solve problems can create entirely different revenue opportunities.
05. More content isn't always the answer.
Sometimes your business needs a better offer.
Sometimes it needs clearer messaging.
Sometimes it needs a stronger sales process.
And sometimes it needs you to stop creating more things and improve what already exists.
06. Artificial intelligence makes business strategy more important, not less.
AI gives us extraordinary capabilities.
But knowing how to use those capabilities to create meaningful customer value requires judgment, direction and strategic thinking.
07. Your business should support your life, not consume it.
I understand what it's like to have ambitions that are much bigger than the amount of time you have available.
That's why I believe in prioritizing meaningful work, building efficient systems and making business decisions that respect the life you're trying to create.
