The strategy behind the design

What you don’t see when you look at a finished website.

You see the beautiful homepage.

The brand colors.

The typography.

The photography.

The carefully designed pages.

You don’t see the audience research behind the messaging.

The offer strategy behind the navigation.

The customer journey behind the calls to action.

The positioning decisions behind the brand.

Or the conversations that helped turn an overwhelming collection of ideas into a cohesive business direction.

But what you don’t immediately see are the business decisions that influenced how those pages were built.

That’s the work that makes my approach different.

When you hire me as your blogging coach and website designer, we’re not simply deciding what your website should look like.

We’re deciding what your website needs to accomplish.

Every design decision should have a business reason behind it.

  • Your homepageshould communicate your value.
  • Your navigationshould help potential customers find what they need.
  • Your contentshould introduce people to your expertise.
  • Your sales pagesshould help customers understand the value of your offers.
  • Your email signup opportunitiesshould give visitors a compelling reason to remain connected.
  • Your website as a wholeshould support the business you’re building.

Beautiful design gets people’s attention.

Strategic design gives that attention somewhere meaningful to go.

Frequently asked questions

Questions before we begin.

Do you only design websites for bloggers?+

My specialty is working with women who want to build online businesses using blogging as part of their marketing and business strategy.

This can include coaches, educators, digital product creators, service providers, personal brands and entrepreneurs.

Do I need to have my business strategy figured out before hiring you?+

No. Business strategy is a central part of the BOSS Business Experience.

We’ll work through your business direction, positioning, offers and audience before developing the website around them.

What platform do you design websites on?+

I specialize in custom Showit website design, with WordPress integration for blogging.

Will my website look like the other websites in your portfolio?+

No. Your website is designed around your individual business, brand identity, audience and goals.

My portfolio demonstrates the quality and range of my work rather than a collection of designs you need to choose from.

What if I already have a website?+

We can explore your existing website, business strategy and goals to determine whether a strategic redesign or a more comprehensive business development experience would make sense.

Is blog coaching included with my website design?+

Private blog coaching is a core component of the BOSS Business Experience.
