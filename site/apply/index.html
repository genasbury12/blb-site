Your personalized business presentation

Imagine sitting down with someone who has already looked at your business and prepared a strategic vision for what it could become.

Your Fast Track presentation is developed around your individual circumstances.

Depending on what your business needs, we’ll explore areas such as:

01

Your current reality versus your desired business

Where are you now? Where do you want to be? What is preventing those two realities from coming together?

We’ll examine your goals, current efforts and existing business foundations.

02

Your hidden revenue leaks

I’ll look for places where potential customers may be losing interest, becoming confused or reaching a dead end before purchasing.

Sometimes the problem isn’t attracting more people.

It’s what happens after those people discover you.

03

Your offer ecosystem

Are your offers competing against each other?

Are you giving customers too many choices?

Is there a clear reason to purchase each offer?

Are you creating opportunities for existing customers to continue buying from you?

We’ll examine how your products and services could work together more strategically.

04

Your brand messaging and positioning

Does your potential customer immediately understand that your business is for her?

Does your messaging communicate a problem she recognizes and a solution she values?

We’ll explore where your communication may need greater specificity, clarity and direction.

05

Your website and customer experience

I’ll consider how your existing website supports your business goals.

Where appropriate, I may prepare visual concepts showing how your homepage, customer pathways or website structure could be improved.

06

Your marketing and sales funnels

We’ll examine how someone discovers your business, develops trust, explores your offers and eventually becomes a customer.

I’ll show you where those connections could become stronger.

07

Your AI implementation opportunities

We’ll explore where artificial intelligence could support your business development, content production, digital product creation or daily operations.

Not simply to create more content, but to make the work you’re already doing more intentional and efficient.

08

Your business development roadmap

I’ll help you visualize the sequence of work that could move your business forward.

What needs to happen first?

What should wait?

What needs to be developed, refined or reconsidered?

And how could those projects support your longer term business goals?

What if you could actually see what needs to change?

There’s a difference between someone telling you that your website needs better messaging and someone showing you how your homepage could be structured differently.

There’s a difference between being told to build an email funnel and seeing how your content, free resources, offers and email sequences could connect.

There’s a difference between being told to simplify your business and seeing which of your offers are competing against each other.

That’s why I incorporate visual examples and personalized strategic concepts into my Fast Track presentations when they’re appropriate for the business.
