OPTIONAL READING BEFORE THE MASTERCLASS

If you’re curious about how I approach building blogging businesses...

There are a few beliefs that influence everything I teach.

  1. Your blog is marketing. It’s not your entire business.

  3. Your expertise becomes more valuable when you understand how to turn it into meaningful customer solutions.
  4. Your website should support your sales strategy rather than simply showcase your content.
