## 1. Phase 2: Approved Program Architecture

### Approved Decision 1: Guided and Personalized
The program supports Build From Scratch and Optimize & Automate. The audit recommends lessons and priorities, but all eight modules are unlocked immediately.

### Approved Decision 2: Layered Curriculum
Each module contains approximately 6 to 10 essential lessons of 5 to 15 minutes, plus optional advanced tutorials, templates and assignments.

### Approved Decision 3: Two Types of Progress
Track learning completion and business implementation milestones separately. Optional evidence may be attached. Feedback depends on purchased tier.

### Approved Decision 4: Eight Module Outcomes
1. Business Foundation: business model, audience, offer direction and messaging.
2. Brand & Website: professional website and customer journey.
3. Content Production Engine: repeatable sales aligned blogging workflow.
4. Marketing & Distribution: sustainable content promotion.
5. Digital Product Development: sellable digital product and delivery method.
6. Email & Sales Systems: working email funnel, checkout and delivery.
7. AI & Automation Hub: tested workflows that reduce repetitive tasks.
8. CEO Productivity System: manageable planning, execution and measurement.

Build From Scratch creates assets. Optimize & Automate improves or replaces existing assets.

### Approved Decision 5: Personalized Six Month Roadmap
Month 1: Clarity & Foundation. Month 2: Brand & Business Home. Month 3: Product & Sales Infrastructure.
Month 4: Content & Visibility. Month 5: Systems & Automation. Month 6: Launch, Optimize & Grow.
The audit personalizes tasks and lessons within the six monthly themes. Work does not reset when a student falls behind.

### Approved Decision 6: Audit First
Immediately after account setup, the 30 question audit is the primary action. Students can save and resume, and still access purchased training before completion. Results include scores, path, top three priorities, starting lesson and roadmap.

### Tier and Access Constraints
Blueprint includes curriculum, audit, templates and dashboard. Accelerator adds six months of group coaching, community, workshops, Hot Seat and two feedback submissions per monthly cycle. Elite adds private coaching, private board, custom roadmap and 12 implementation hours.
Core curriculum access lasts 12 months, with approved membership extensions. Installments are committed purchases.

### Pending Phase 3
Finalize lesson identifiers and precise audit to lesson mapping for Modules 2 through 8; produce scripts, worksheets and recordings; test recommendations and permissions.

## 2. BOSS Business Audit: 30 Questions and Scoring

### Business Foundation
Q1. Which best describes your blogging business? A. I haven't started. B. I have a blog but no clear business. C. I have a blog and offer but inconsistent sales. D. I make sales and want to improve or automate.
Q2. How clearly can you describe your customer? A. No defined customer. B. General audience. C. Clear customer and problems. D. Detailed customer understanding used in marketing.
Q3. How clear is your business promise? A. No promise. B. General idea. C. Clear problem and result. D. Consistent message supported by feedback.
Q4. Do you have a digital product or offer? A. No idea. B. Unvalidated idea. C. Defined offer in development. D. Offer for sale with sales.

### Brand & Website
Q5. Website status? A. None. B. In development. C. Live and functional. D. Maintained and optimized.
Q6. Brand consistency? A. None. B. Some inconsistent elements. C. Mostly consistent. D. Documented and consistently applied.
Q7. Can visitors understand your offer? A. No live website. B. Unclear offer. C. Offer understandable. D. Clear journey to purchasing.

### Content Production Engine
Q8. How do you choose topics? A. No content. B. Whatever comes to mind. C. Audience problems. D. Buyer needs, search intent and offers.
Q9. How consistently do you publish? A. Not yet. B. Occasionally. C. Realistic schedule. D. Reliable documented workflow.
Q10. Does content connect to products? A. No content or product. B. No offer links. C. Some calls to action. D. Intentional path to offers or signup.
Q11. Is your content process documented? A. No. B. Rough routine. C. Checklist. D. Documented templates and QA.

### Marketing & Distribution
Q12. How do you promote? A. Not yet. B. Occasionally. C. Active on one or two channels. D. Planned distribution.
Q13. How do you choose channels? A. None. B. Trying many. C. Based on audience. D. Evaluate qualified traffic and buyers.
Q14. What happens after publishing? A. Not publishing. B. Hope for discovery. C. Some promotion. D. Repeatable repurposing workflow.

### Digital Product Development
Q15. Product status? A. None. B. Idea or outline. C. In creation. D. Complete and for sale.
Q16. How have you validated demand? A. No research. B. Assumptions. C. Customer problem research. D. Direct feedback or purchase evidence.
Q17. How defined is the product outcome? A. No product. B. Vague topic. C. Buyer, outcome and scope clear. D. Refined with feedback.
Q18. How ready is delivery? A. Not planned. B. Idea only. C. Method and instructions. D. Tested purchase and access.

### Email & Sales Systems
Q19. How do you collect subscribers? A. No list. B. Platform without effective signup. C. Connected opt in. D. Monitored lead system.
Q20. What happens after signup? A. Nothing. B. One welcome email. C. Planned sequence. D. Relevant automated nurture and offer sequence.
Q21. Is checkout ready? A. No. B. Researching. C. Functional. D. Fully tested.
Q22. How do you track sales? A. No tracking. B. Occasional checks. C. Track leads and conversions. D. Review and optimize regularly.

### AI & Automation Hub
Q23. How do you use AI? A. Not yet. B. Occasionally. C. Clear repeatable tasks. D. Documented workflows with review.
Q24. How much work is automated? A. None. B. One or two tasks. C. Several reliable tasks. D. Connected workflows.
Q25. How do you quality check? A. Not using AI. B. Rarely review. C. Review and test. D. Documented QA and error recovery.
Q26. How connected are your tools? A. Not selected. B. Manual transfers. C. Some integrations. D. Reliable documented integrations.

### CEO Productivity System
Q27. How do you set weekly priorities? A. No plan. B. Lists without focus. C. Goals based priorities. D. Consistent planned execution.
Q28. How do you track unfinished work? A. Memory. B. Scattered notes. C. Central system. D. Reliable reviews and next actions.
Q29. How realistic is your workload? A. No routine. B. Often overplan. C. Plan around available time. D. Consistently adjust priorities.
Q30. How do you review performance? A. Never. B. Occasionally. C. Monthly. D. Regular results based decisions.

### Scoring
A = 0, B = 1, C = 2, D = 3. Category score is earned points divided by maximum applicable points. Overall score is the average of the eight category percentages, so categories have equal weight. Scores reflect self reported system maturity, not projected revenue.

Do not allow Not Applicable for essential business assets. If a question is genuinely irrelevant, exclude it only under documented eligibility rules; a business not yet using AI is considered not started.

### Path Assignment
Build From Scratch if Q1, Q4, Q5 or Q15 is A or B. Optimize & Automate if Q1, Q4, Q5 and Q15 are all C or D. Allow a manual override while retaining audit history.

### Priority Logic
Flag foundational gaps: Q2 or Q3 A/B; offer Q4 A/B; product Q15 or Q17 A/B; sales Q21 A/B; delivery Q18 A/B; website Q5 or Q7 A/B; email Q19 or Q20 A/B.

Select three distinct actionable priorities. Resolve essential gaps in order: clarity, product, sales, website, email, content/marketing, automation, productivity. Then fill remaining slots with lowest scoring categories; break ties by dependency order.

### Recommended Starting Module
Foundation to Module 1; website to Module 2; content to Module 3; marketing to Module 4; product to Module 5; email/sales to Module 6; AI to Module 7; productivity to Module 8.

For Module 1, Q1 low recommends lessons 1 and 2; Q2 low lesson 3; Q3 low lessons 6 and 7; Q4 low lessons 4 and 5; Q16 low lesson 4; Q17 low lessons 5 and 6. Other lesson IDs pending Phase 3.

### Audit Report and Retakes
Include name, date, path, scores, three strengths, three priorities with reasons, recommended lesson and personalized six month roadmap. Preserve scoring version, previous results and completed work. Recommendations must be explainable and must not invent metrics.

## 3. Student Experience and Portal Blueprint

### Purchase and First Login
Blueprint and Accelerator use checkout then secure account creation. Elite uses application and acceptance before private checkout.
Welcome screen leads with Start My Business Audit; students may explore purchased training without completing the audit.

### Audit Experience
Eight short sections, 30 questions, visible progress, autosave, backward navigation and resume. Results provide a readiness profile, path, top priorities, starting lesson and PDF report.

### Dashboard
One recommended next action receives visual priority. Show top three audit priorities, learning completion, implementation milestones, current roadmap month and relevant tier support.

### Main Navigation
My Dashboard; My Training; Implementation Library; My Business Audit; Coaching & Community for entitled tiers; My Account. Elite also receives My Elite Workspace.

### Lesson Experience
Video, learning objective, assignment, relevant downloads, external AI assistant prompts, mark lesson complete, next lesson. Optional advanced tutorials do not count toward essential completion.

### Implementation and Coaching
Milestones are tracked separately from lesson completion. Blueprint self reports. Accelerator and Elite can submit eligible work for feedback under tier limits.
Group coaching Wednesdays at 1 PM Eastern. Hot Seat second Tuesday at 1 PM Eastern. Workshops first and third Mondays. Elite includes private sessions and a private implementation board.

### Tier Access and Edge Cases
Respect purchased entitlements, coaching periods, course access, payment grace and suspension. Audit exit saves answers. Retakes preserve prior reports. Learning path changes never erase progress. Unfinished milestones remain visible.

### Admin Workspace
Manage student profiles, entitlements, audits, lessons, progress, feedback queue, workshops, sessions, Elite requests, hours and payment issues. Protect private data by role.

### Design and Technical Handoff
Brand palette: dusty rose #E8C8CB, muted mauve #A56C78, ivory #F7EEE9, plum #65474F. Premium editorial serif headings and readable sans serif body.
Planned implementation: Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase authentication/database/storage with row level security, Stripe webhooks and access control. Test purchase, audit, dashboard, tier restrictions, payment failures and expiry.
