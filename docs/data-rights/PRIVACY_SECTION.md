## Your data in the member portal

### What we keep about you

When you join The BOSS Business System, the member portal keeps:

- **Your account:** your email address, the name you choose to show, your time zone and your accessibility preference.
- **Your purchases:** the program or membership you bought, the price, your payment plan, payment dates and status, and reference numbers from Stripe, our payment processor. We never see or store your card number; Stripe handles that.
- **Your access:** which parts of the program you can use and until when.
- **Your work in the program:** your business audit answers and results, your roadmap, the lessons and milestones you mark complete (and any files you attach), and every worksheet answer.
- **What you send us:** coaching questions, Hot Seat applications (including your recording consent), feedback submissions and their files, and your community posts, replies and reports.
- **Elite clients also:** your application, weekly check ins, requests, shared files, private session schedule and the record of implementation hours.
- **A record of changes:** our team's actions in the portal (for example a change to your access) are written to an activity log, along with a record each time your data is downloaded or a deletion is requested. These records never contain your worksheet answers or other content.

### Who can see it

You can see everything about yourself. A coach can see the work of members assigned to them, but never your email address, and cannot download your data. Our administrators can see member records to run the program and support you, and every download they make of your data is recorded.

### Download a copy of your data

Go to **My Account**, then **Your data**, then **Download all my data**. You get one ZIP file with everything the portal keeps about you, a PDF of every worksheet you completed, your latest audit report, and the files you uploaded. You can download it as often as you like, once every 10 minutes. We record the date and size of each download, never what is in it. Notes our coaches keep for their own use are not part of your download.

This stays available after your program access ends, until your account is deleted (see below).

### Delete your account and data

Go to **My Account**, then **Your data**, then **Delete my account and data**. The page explains what will happen, and you confirm by typing DELETE. Your account is deleted within 30 days, and we email you when it is done. Until then you can still sign in, download your data, or cancel the request.

**What is deleted:** your account and sign in, your profile, your audit, roadmap, progress, milestones and worksheet answers, your coaching questions, Hot Seat applications and feedback, your Elite workspace, every file you uploaded, and your customer record at Stripe.

**Your community posts and replies** stay in place so other members' conversations still make sense, but their words are replaced with a note that they were removed, and your name with "Deleted member".

**What we keep, and why:**

- **The record of your purchases** (product, amount, dates and payment status), which we need for tax and refunds, for 7 years. Your name and email are removed from it and replaced by an anonymous code.
- **Our activity log**, where your name, email and account are replaced by the same anonymous code.
- **Stripe** keeps its own records of past payments under its own privacy policy.

### When your access ends

Your account and everything in it stay for **12 months** after your program access ends, so you can come back or download your data. We email you **30 days** and **7 days** before we delete it. If you join again before then, nothing is deleted. After 12 months, the account is deleted the same way as if you had asked.

### How long we keep things

| What | How long |
|---|---|
| Your account and your work | While you have access, then 12 months |
| A deletion you ask for | Carried out within 30 days |
| Purchase records (anonymized after deletion) | 7 years |
| Activity log (anonymized after deletion) | Kept |
