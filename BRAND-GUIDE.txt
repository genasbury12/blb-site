1. THE BRAND IN ONE BREATH
=====================================================================
Boss Lady Bloggers (BLB) is Gen's blog coaching + business education
brand for women. Founded 2017. Positioning line:
  "Blog coaching for women who want to make money, not just publish
   content."
Tagline (script, keep lowercase): beautiful blogs, built to sell
Footer line: "Blog coaching, business strategy, digital products,
and smarter systems for women who want to build businesses that sell."
Core idea said everywhere: "Your blog is not your business. It's how
people find your business." / "Build the business. Let your blog
market it."
Flagship offer: The BOSS Business System (three tiers: Blueprint,
Accelerator, Elite). Supporting offers: The Productive Blogger
Method, The Project Club, The Blogging Ladies membership, 1:1 blog
coaching by the call.

LOGO RULES
  - The wordmark is typographic: Fraunces 500 at high optical size,
    the word "Lady" ALWAYS italic and ALWAYS Boss Pink (cream
    background) or Rose (ink background). Never all one color.
  - Clear space: at least the height of the letter B on all sides.
  - Never stretch, outline, shadow, or recolor outside the palette.
  - On photos: use the ink version over light areas inside a cream
    rounded card, never raw over a busy photo.
  - JPGs have baked backgrounds (cream #FBF6EE or ink #2E1F27). Match
    the canvas you place them on, or ask for PNG/SVG versions.

  Cream text     #D8CBC3  body text on dark

RULES
  - Buttons are ALWAYS the hot pink gradient (bright pink). The deep
    #A93F63 is for text accents, never buttons (client decision,
    Oct 27 2026 audit).
  - One pink button per zone. If everything is pink, nothing is.
  - Text on cream/tint/blush = Ink. Text on ink = Cream text, with
    bold words in white and accents in Rose.
  - Gold is for accents and script only, never body text.

4. TYPOGRAPHY
=====================================================================
Google Fonts embed:
https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Fraunces:ital,opsz,wght@0,9..144,300..700;1,9..144,300..600&family=Karla:wght@400;500;600;700&family=Ephesis&display=swap

  DISPLAY: Fraunces, weight 500, line-height 1.12, letter-spacing
    -.01em. Headlines mix roman + italic; italic words take Boss
    Pink (light bg) or Rose (dark bg).
    H1 clamp(2.3rem, 5.4vw, 4rem). Section H2 clamp(2.1rem,4.6vw,3.4rem).
  BODY: Karla 400, 1.03rem, line-height 1.7. Bold = Karla 700.
    Key sentences are BOLDED for skimmers; on dark bg bold = white.
  SCRIPT: Ephesis, gold (or rose on dark), 22-46px equivalent. For
    asides and taglines only. Max one script line per section. Never
    body text.
  LABELS (eyebrows): Karla 700, .72rem, letterspacing .26em,
    uppercase, deep plum (rose on dark), with a gold sparkle (2726)
    before. NO numbered "01/02" circles (client removed them).

5. SIGNATURE DESIGN ELEMENTS (what makes a page look like BLB)
=====================================================================
  - Alternating section canvases: paper / tint / ink / blush, so no
    two neighboring sections match.
  - Giant italic outline watermark words behind sections (1.5px
    stroke, pink at ~13% on light, cream at ~10% on dark).
  - Scrolling marquee bands (ink, gold, blush) with heart separators.
  - Spinning circular-text badges around a gradient heart/arrow.
  - Sticky-note/polaroid photo cards with slight rotations and
    script captions; gold "sticker" pills rotated a few degrees.
  - Receipt-style proof cards with perforated edges.
  - Hand-drawn feel: gold underline SVG squiggles, sparkles (2726),
    dashed gold borders for "free" things.
  - Arch-top (230px radius) photo frames for portraits.
  - Checklists with gradient check circles; crossed-out old-way vs
    bold new-way comparison rows.
  - Script numerals (Ephesis) for CONTENT lists only.
MOTION (all guarded by prefers-reduced-motion):
  - Scroll-in reveals (fade/rise, stagger), split-word hero
    headlines, count-up stats, tilt cards, magnetic buttons, scroll
    progress bar, confetti on thank-you pages.
  - Reveal system rule: never leave clip-path:inset(0) on resting
    elements; overflow:hidden text wrappers need descender padding.

6. VOICE + COPY RULES (current era)
=====================================================================
  - Best-friend-who-did-it voice. Short sentences. Plain words.
    Truth even when it stings, then what to do about it.
  - Current site copy is warmer-professional: "Babe"/"girl" RARELY
    now (the BOSS-era copy uses none). Match the copy doc given.
  - No em dashes. No "not X, it's Y" constructions. No lists of
    three for rhythm. Max one censored swear per page (h*ll, d*mn)
    and only if the source copy has it.
  - Bold the sentences a skimmer must not miss.
  - Compliance lines stay verbatim: "Business results vary. No
    earnings are guaranteed." / "My results are my own..."

7. CTA + PRICING RULES (client decision, Nov 13 2026)
=====================================================================
  - THE primary CTA sitewide is "Book a Free Call" -> /book-a-call
    (the free Blog Monetization Fast Track call, Calendly embedded).
  - NO offer prices anywhere on the public site. Pricing is
    discussed on the free call. Tier/package cards list contents,
    never amounts. "Payment plans available - pricing on your free
    call" is the approved phrasing.
  - Secondary CTAs allowed: "See The BOSS Business System"
    (/boss-business-system), free training signup
    (/free-blogging-masterclass, Flodesk form).
  - Exception: income PROOF receipts (screenshots) stay. They are
    evidence, not price lists.
  - Exception (client, Nov 13 2026): /blog-coaching (The Blog
    Profit Intensive) is the ONE page that shows its price:
    $897 pay in full or 3 x $333, in the enrollment card. Its
    CTAs still go to /book-a-call.

8. PROOF RULES (binding, from the Proof Bank)
=====================================================================
  - Never invent testimonials, numbers, or results. Empty beats fake.
  - Income receipts are SAMPLES, never totals/monthly/yearly claims.
    $34,481 may only be framed "paid through my invoices".
  - Blurred proof files only (repo /proof). Never originals.
  - Client email quotes may run verbatim but WITHOUT names until the
    client approves hers. Zoom-call lines are paraphrase: tell as
    stories only, never quotation marks.
  - Approved proof assets (repo /proof via raw.githubusercontent,
    same branch): proof-first-stripe-payouts-2020-2021.jpg ($18.52

11. QUICK DO / DON'T
=====================================================================
DO: alternate canvases - bold the money sentences - gold script
asides - real receipts - arch photos - bright pink gradient buttons -
"Book a Free Call" as the ask - reduced-motion fallbacks.
DON'T: list offer prices - fake proof or countdowns - numbered
section kickers - em dashes - more than one script line per section -
JavaScript on blog templates - Lady in the wordmark un-italicized.
