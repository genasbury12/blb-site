=====================================================================
2. COLORS
=====================================================================
CORE PALETTE
  Paper cream    #FBF6EE   Every page and email background.
                           NEVER pure white.
  Ink plum       #2E1F27   Body text, dark sections, footer.
                           NEVER pure black.
  Boss pink      #A93F63   THE action color. Buttons, links,
                           accents, anything meant to be clicked.
  Deep plum      #7C2B48   Hover states, small caps labels,
                           text on blush backgrounds.
  Gold           #BC8A47   Script accents, dividers, doodles,
                           flourishes. Warmth, not bling.

SUPPORTING SHADES
  Blush          #F0DCE2   Soft highlight backgrounds, pills,
                           category tags.
  Tint sand      #F4EBDE   Alternate section background (used to
                           break up long stretches of cream).
  Muted mauve    #6E5C63   Secondary text, captions, fine print.

EXTRAS THAT APPEAR IN THE SYSTEM
  Cream text on ink: #D8CBC3 (footer paragraph text)
  White #FFFFFF: card backgrounds only, never page backgrounds.

PAIRING RULES
  - Backgrounds rotate: paper -> tint -> ink -> paper. Never two
    dark sections back to back.
  - One pink button per zone. If everything is pink, nothing is.
  - Text on cream/tint/blush = Ink. Text on ink = Paper cream.
  - Gold is for accents and script only, never body text.
  - Borders on light backgrounds: Ink at 14-35% opacity
    (rgba(46,31,39,.16) for cards, .35 for inputs).


=====================================================================
3. TYPOGRAPHY
=====================================================================
THREE FONTS, THREE JOBS (all free on Google Fonts):

FRAUNCES - the display serif. Headlines, big numbers, quotes.
  Weight: 500 only. Italic for emphasis words and the wordmark
  "Lady". Headline letter-spacing: -0.01em. Line height 1.05-1.15.
  Sizes on the site: h1 40-64px, h2 30-45px, h3 20-24px
  (fluid with clamp()).

KARLA - the workhorse sans. Body text, buttons, labels, nav.
  Weights: 400 (body), 700 (bold, buttons, labels).
  Body: 16-17px, line height 1.65-1.75.
  CAPS LABELS (eyebrows, buttons, nav): 700 weight, 10-14px,
  letter-spacing .16em-.24em, all uppercase.

EPHESIS - the handwritten script. Accent lines only.
  One line at a time ("so close, babe", "pop your email in").
  22-46px. In Gold or Boss pink. NEVER for body text, never
  more than one script line per section.

GOOGLE FONTS EMBED:
https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Fraunces:ital,opsz,wght@0,9..144,500;1,9..144,500&family=Karla:wght@400;700&family=Ephesis&display=swap


=====================================================================
4. SHAPES, SHADOWS, AND TEXTURE
=====================================================================
CORNERS
  Buttons + pills: fully round (border-radius 60px)
  Cards: 20-26px
  Large feature cards / dark bands: 26-30px
  Photos: 20-24px, or the signature ARCH:
    border-radius: 46vw 46vw 24px 24px (rounded top, square-ish
    bottom, like a window)

SHADOWS (soft but dramatic, always warm-toned)
  Cards:    0 26px 50px -26px rgba(46,31,39,.38)
  Features: 0 34px 64px -30px rgba(46,31,39,.45)
  Pink pop: 0 18px 34px -14px rgba(169,63,99,.65) (buttons)

SIGNATURE DECORATIVE ELEMENTS
  - Giant outlined words behind sections: Fraunces italic,
    huge, transparent fill, 1px stroke of pink or cream at
    10-18% opacity
  - Hand-drawn SVG doodles: arrows, underline squiggles, in Gold
    or Boss pink, stroke width 2.5-3.5, round caps
  - Spinning circular text badges (SVG textPath, 14-15s rotation)
  - Sliding text marquees: Fraunces caps with sparkle
    separators (the four-pointed star ✦, entity &#10022;)
  - Floating stickers: white or blush pills, slight rotation
    (-7deg to 5deg), caps text
  - Polaroids: white frame, 12-14px padding, 40-52px bottom,
    script caption, slight rotation
  - Sparkles: ✦ in pink or gold, never emoji stars

MOTION RULES
  - Subtle and constant beats loud and once: slow marquees,
    gentle floats, soft sheens
  - Hover: lift (-3 to -8px translateY) + deepen shadow
  - Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion


=====================================================================
5. BUTTONS
=====================================================================
PRIMARY (the pink pill)
  Background Boss pink, white text, Karla 700, 12-14px,
  letter-spacing .18em, uppercase, padding ~17px 36px,
  radius 60px, pink-tinted shadow.
  Hover: background Ink, lift 3px.
  Copy style: action + arrow. "Book your call →"
  "Unlock the free courses →" "See the program →"

SECONDARY (the outline pill)
  Transparent background, 1px Ink border, Ink text.
  On dark: cream border at 40-50% opacity, cream text.
  Hover: fills (pink on light, cream on dark).

RULE: one primary per section. Secondary supports, never competes.


=====================================================================
6. VOICE
=====================================================================
  - Warm, blunt, confident. A big sister who built the thing.
  - Short punchy lines. One idea per paragraph.
  - "babe" sparingly - once or twice per page or email, max.
  - Sell with certainty, never pressure. The PS is the soft sell.
  - Say "payment plans available" wherever a price appears.
  - NO EM DASHES. Use periods, commas, or the word "and".
  - Script lines are little winks: "so close, babe",
    "all three are kind of a big deal", "no pitch, just a plan".
