And so the thing on your screen looks nothing like the thing in your head.

The payment plan splits it across the program so it's never all at once: three payments of $1,000. If the first payment is still out of reach, book the call anyway and tell me. I'd rather have the conversation than have you assume the answer.

How much time will this take me?

An hour a week on our call, plus questionnaire time, which is front-loaded into the first week or two. That's it. I'm doing the building.

You use AI. Will my site feel like AI made it?

No, and I understand the worry. AI doesn't decide anything about your site. Your questionnaires do, your voice does, and I do. What AI changed is speed. What if I get to the end and it doesn't make money?

I can't promise sales, and I'd be careful with anyone who does. What I can promise is that you'll finish with the thing almost every stuck blogger is missing: something worth selling, words that sell it, and a site designed to convert. What happens after depends on you showing up, which is exactly why I document the whole process. You're not just getting a website. You're learning how it was built.

No, and I'd rather you read that here than hear it on a call. This is done-with-you work that starts immediately and I only want women in it who are sure.
