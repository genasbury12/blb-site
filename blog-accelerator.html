Sound familiar?

You've watched the YouTube videos. You've saved the Pinterest pins.

You've got a notes app full of blog names and a Canva folder full of logos you never used. And every time you sit down to actually build the thing, you end up stuck on the same questions:

  • What should I even blog about?
  • Is WordPress or Showit or Squarespace the "right" one?
  • Do I need a logo first? A niche first? Ten posts first?
  • How do bloggers actually make money? Is it ads? Affiliates? Something else?

So you close the laptop. Again. And tell yourself you'll start for real next month.

What nobody tells you

The blog isn't the hard part. The order is.

Most new bloggers are taught to do it backwards. Pick a pretty theme. Write 30 posts. Join every affiliate program. Wait for Google to send you traffic. Hope money shows up someday.

And now there's a new twist. Google answers questions with an AI summary before anyone clicks. Everyone and their cousin is pumping out AI-written posts. So the traffic you were told to wait for? It's getting harder to get every month.

No wonder everyone's saying "blogging is dead." If you do it the old way, they're kind of right.

So you spend a year writing for nobody. You make $4.12 in affiliate commissions. You decide blogging "doesn't work," or worse, that you don't work. And meanwhile, life keeps going:

  • Your blog time is stolen in 20-minute chunks between work, kids, dinner, and laundry
  • Every free hour you get, you spend researching instead of building
  • You keep buying courses, and the courses keep sitting there unopened
  • You're tired of being the friend who's "thinking about starting a blog"

You don't need more information. You've got plenty of that. You need someone to tell you what to do this week, and then help you do it.

Now imagine

Three months from now.

Your blog is live. It's gorgeous. It looks like you, not like a template every other blogger is using.

There's a digital product on it. Your ebook, your course, or your membership. Not a someday idea. A real sales page with a button.

There's a freebie collecting email addresses while you're at work. There's a plan for what to write next, and every post points people toward what you sell.

And when someone asks what you do, you don't say "oh, I'm trying to start a blog." You send them the link.

My story

I blogged for two years and made nothing. Then I changed one thing.

I started blogging in 2017. I did everything the gurus said. I wrote the posts. I pinned the pins. I added the affiliate links. Two years. Nothing.

Then I flipped the order. I decided what I was selling first. I designed my website to sell that product. I built the blog to send people to it. I made $4,000 in the next four months. Same me. Same amount of time. Different order.

And that order matters even more now that AI is everywhere. It never depended on Google traffic. It depends on you: your voice, your email list, and your digital products. AI can answer a question. It can't be the person your reader trusts and buys from.

Blogging isn't dead. The traffic-only way of blogging is. That order is what I've been teaching ever since. I've signed one-on-one contracts for $7,000 and up to build it for bloggers. And now it's the backbone of the Blog Build Club.

The plan

Here's what we build, month by month

M1

Your digital product and your message.

This is the month everyone wants to skip. It's also the reason most blogs never make money. So we start here. Find your person: who you help, what they're stuck on, and what they'd happily pay to fix. Plan your digital product: an ebook, a course, a membership, or printables, whichever fits your life and skills right now. Write your message: the one sentence that tells people exactly what you do, so they get it in five seconds.

M2

Your brand and your website.

Now that you know what you're selling, we design a website whose whole job is to sell it. Your brand: colors, fonts, and a look that feels like you on your best day. Your website copy: homepage, about page, and sales page, written to lead people straight to your digital product. Your Showit website: a custom design with a sales page for your digital products, built to turn readers into buyers. It's the same kind of website design I've built for clients at $7,000 and up.

M3

Your freebie, email list, and content plan.

This is the month your blog starts working for you. Your freebie: one helpful free thing that gets the right people onto your email list. Your email list: set up and connected, so new subscribers hear from you automatically. Your content map: blog posts planned around your digital product, so every post has a purpose. Then: launch. Your blog goes live. You send the link to everybody.

M4-6

One growth project a month.

Launching is the beginning. After launch, we pick one project each month that grows your list or your sales. Think: a launch to your email list, a new freebie, or your first sales email series. Same weekly calls, same support.

Questions bloggers ask before they join

Fair questions, honest answers

I've never blogged before. Is that okay?

Yes. The Club is built for beginners. We start at zero, and you'll never get a step without an explanation.

Is this blog coaching or a course?

It's blog coaching. You get weekly calls with me, feedback on your actual blog, and help with your digital product and website design. Not a pile of videos to watch alone.

I don't know what I'd sell. Can I still join?

Absolutely. That's Month 1. Most people come in without a product idea. We figure it out together.

I'm so busy. How much time does this take?

You get one assignment a week, built to fit around a full life. Plan on a few focused hours a week, plus the weekly call.

Do I need Showit?

Yes. You'll buy and keep your own Showit account, domain, and email marketing account. That way, everything stays yours.

Do I need professional photos?

Yes, you'll need photos of yourself for your website. You'll need them on time so your launch stays on track.

Do you build the website for me?

We design it together, in order, with blog coaching every week. You get my web design eye on every page, so it's built to sell your digital products. If you want more done-for-you help and private calls, look at the Fast Track Private Build Club.

Do I need a big following?

No. You don't need any followers to start. Your freebie and email list are how you start growing.

Is blogging even worth it now that there's AI?

Yes, if you do it the way we do it here. Blogging that depends on Google traffic and ads is getting harder. Blogging that builds trust, grows an email list, and sells your own digital products still works, because AI can't be you.

There are so many bloggers already. Is there room for me?

Yes. More generic content makes a clear, personal blog with a real digital product stand out more, not less. You don't need to beat everyone. You need the right people to find you and trust you.

Can I use AI to help build my blog?

Sure. Use it to brainstorm and draft faster. Just don't let it replace your voice. Your voice is what people are buying.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. The Club is built as a 6-month program because that's how long real results take, so if you leave early, you won't see the full results. You keep everything you built. Payments already made are not refunded.
