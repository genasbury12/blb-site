About me

Hi, I'm Genasys. Just an ordinary mom, going all in on the dream again.

I run Boss Lady Bloggers, I've been doing this since 2017, and I'm not about to pretend I have it all figured out. I've got a kid, a day job, a big dream, and a stubborn belief that you can build something of your own without faking your way there. Let me tell you how I got here.

Real talk

Let me be honest with you from the jump.

I'm not writing this from a beach house with a green juice and a perfect life. I'm a mom with a day job, a little one underfoot, and a business I refuse to give up on. Right now we're saving for a house and I'm building this thing again in the margins of a very full life, out loud, no highlight reel. If you've ever felt like everyone else has it figured out except you, pull up a chair. Same, babe.

How it started

It started with a Pinterest search and a basement.

I didn't want to go to school. Deep down I knew a 9-5 wasn't built for me. So one night I was scrolling Pinterest looking up how to make money from home, and I found blogging. I was living in my aunt's basement at the time. Big dreams, not much else.

I started a lifestyle blog called Genasys Lifestyle on a DIY WordPress setup with a little theme I could barely wrangle. It took me days to figure WordPress out, I hated how my site looked, and I quickly realized making money this way was so much harder than the internet made it sound. But I kept going anyway.

What changed everything

Business first. Blog second. Always.

A blog by itself doesn't pay you. A business does. Your blog is the marketing tool that brings the right people to your door so they buy your offers, book your services, or reach out for coaching. The day I flipped that order, digital products mixed with coaching and services, everything started to work. Now that's exactly what I teach.

What I believe

The beliefs I build everything on.

This is the stuff that shapes how I coach, how I design, and how I run my own business.

1

Business first, blogging second

Your blog is the marketing tool that drives traffic to your offers. Not the whole plan, the engine.

2

Marketing is everything

The best offer in the world stays invisible without it. Marketing always matters, full stop.

3

Gorgeous sites that sell

Pretty is lovely. Pretty plus powerful, persuasive words is what actually makes you money.

4

Offers before everything else

Nail what you're selling first. Then brand messaging, branding, copywriting, all of it.

5

Build it right the first time

Even when it takes a little longer to launch. No flimsy foundations, no shortcuts you'll regret.

6

Stay true to you

You can write what you believe in and sell something too. Those have never been opposites.

And the fuel behind all of it? Strategy, organization, a real business mindset, grit, and a whole lot of passion.

Where I draw the line

What I flat-out refuse to do.

I won't let you start a DIY WordPress blog, stuff it full of affiliate links, and cross your fingers that someday it'll magically make enough to quit your job and live some luxurious laptop life. Because for most people, that day just never comes. I've watched it not come. That old "post and hope" way of blogging is exactly why so many talented women give up. Not on my watch.

Why me, why us

Who I'm here for, and why we'll click.

I help women who are just starting and already know one thing for sure: they do not want to waste years trying to figure this out alone. You're the one who'd rather invest, hire a real blog coach, and do it right the first time than spin your wheels for free and hope it works out.

And I get you because I've been you. The basement. The ugly first site. The day job that pays the bills but isn't the dream. I'm not coaching you from some mountaintop. I'm right here doing it alongside you.

The honest part

2025 knocked me flat. Here's the truth.

Last year was rough. We left our rental in Michigan and moved in with family to save for a house, and it didn't go the way we hoped. So we packed up again and landed at my brother and sister in law's place here in Ohio. I lost the daily support that made running my business possible, so I took a day job to keep us steady.

It was good for a season. But it's not me. This, the business, the blog, helping women like you, this is the thing I light up for. So while we save for our house, I'm going all in again, figuring it out with my kid on my hip the way I always have.

I'm telling you all of this so you know exactly who you're trusting. Not a polished guru. A real woman, mid-climb, who's done this since 2017 and isn't quitting.
