My story

Hi, I'm Gen. And I didn't figure this out overnight.

I started blogging in 2017. Like so many women starting their first blogs, I thought the formula was pretty straightforward.

Pick a niche⟶Build a website⟶Write blog posts⟶Get traffic⟶Make money

So that's what I focused on. I spent two years trying to make blogging work the way I'd been taught. I learned about content, traffic, and affiliate marketing. But I wasn't building the kind of income I wanted. And eventually I had to face something I hadn't understood when I started.

I'd learned how to run a blog. I hadn't learned how to build a business.

A business needs someone specific to serve. A problem worth solving. Something people can buy. A way to attract those people. And a clear path from interested visitor to paying customer. Your blog is part of that. But it isn't the whole thing.

Once I understood the difference, I started approaching everything differently. Instead of asking what I should publish next, I started asking what people actually needed help with. Instead of relying on traffic to somehow become income, I focused on creating offers, building websites with a purpose, and learning how to sell.

And over time, Boss Lady Bloggers became a multi six figure business.

The real backdrop

A lot of that business was built in my aunt's basement, during my son's nap times.

Not in some perfect office with eight uninterrupted hours to work. I was a single mom, trying to build a business around a life that didn't exactly leave me with unlimited time. I knew what it meant to sit down at my laptop with a hundred things that needed doing and no idea how much I'd actually get finished before my son woke up.

And I knew what it felt like to want more. More financial independence. More control over my schedule. More time to be present for my son without giving up on the business I was building.

So when I talk about wanting a business that gives you freedom, I'm not talking about sitting on a beach with a laptop for an Instagram photo. I'm talking about:

  • Being able to make decisions about your own day
  • Working from home
  • Taking a break when you need one
  • Having something you've built that doesn't depend on someone else giving you permission to succeed

That's the kind of business I wanted. And it's the reason I care so much about helping women build businesses that make sense for their actual lives.

systems The second lesson

But building a successful business taught me another lesson.

Making money is one part of the equation. Running the business without letting it take over your life is another.

As Boss Lady Bloggers grew, I found myself juggling clients, content, marketing, websites, offers, projects, and a growing list of things I wanted to create. I became obsessed with productivity tools. I wanted to know how to organize everything, make better decisions, and stop spending so much time figuring out what to work on.

And when AI tools became more capable, I started experimenting with ways to use them to build and manage business systems. Not just to write another blog post. But to organize projects, plan business goals, build dashboards, document workflows, and reduce repetitive work. And something clicked.

Building a profitable business and building a business that's easier to run should go together.

You need strategy so you're working on things that matter. And you need systems so you're not responsible for remembering, planning, and manually managing every little thing. That's why I teach both.

believe What I believe

A few things I believe about building an online business.

1

Your blog is marketing, not your entire business.

Your content should help the right people discover you and understand how you can help them.

2

Understand what you're selling before chasing traffic.

You don't need to know every detail on day one. But you do need a business direction that goes beyond publishing content and hoping for the best.

3

A beautiful website should do more than look beautiful.

Your messaging, design, and customer experience should make it easy for people to understand what you offer and why it matters.

4

AI should help you do meaningful work, not simply produce more content.

The point isn't to automate everything. It's to make the right work easier to manage and execute.

5

A business should give you more choices, not consume every minute of your life.

You still have to do the work. But that work should have a purpose.
