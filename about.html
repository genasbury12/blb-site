What I believe

Things I'll say out loud that most blog coaches won't

tap a card to flip it
01

Your blog isn't your business. It's your marketing.

flip me →

The blog is how people find you and decide they like you. The offer is how you get paid. Build the blog without the offer and you've built a beautiful machine pointing at nothing.

02

Most "how to start a blog" articles exist to sell you hosting.

flip me →

That's the business model. The writer earns a commission when you click through and sign up. So the same advice gets recycled from post to post, usually written by people who aren't making much from their own blogs.

03

Ads are not worth it.

flip me →

They're distracting, they pull readers away from you, and they pay pennies. Affiliate links are fine on the side. Neither one should be the plan.

04

Pretty and profitable aren't opposites.

flip me →

Somewhere along the way women got told to pick one. You can have a site that's genuinely beautiful and built to convert. That's the only kind I make.
