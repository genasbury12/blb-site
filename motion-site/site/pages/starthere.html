Frequently asked questions

I’m a complete beginner. Where should I start?

Start with my free Futureproof Your Blog masterclass to understand my approach to building blogging businesses.

If you want structured business education afterward, explore Blogging Ladies.

If you’d prefer professional help building your business from the beginning, explore The BOSS Business Experience.

I’ve already been blogging for years. Will your education still be relevant?

Yes. My approach focuses on developing the business behind your blog, including your offers, messaging, customer acquisition and sales systems.

Established bloggers can use these strategies to evaluate and restructure what they’ve already built.

What is the difference between Blogging Ladies and The BOSS Academy?

Blogging Ladies contains my signature BOSS Business System, which teaches the connected foundations behind building a blogging business.

The BOSS Academy contains specialized courses focused on developing particular business skills and capabilities.

What is the difference between the free masterclass and The Futureproof Blogger course?

The free masterclass introduces my perspective on the future of blogging and business ownership.

The Futureproof Blogger explores those concepts in greater depth through a dedicated educational experience.

Do I have to purchase a course before working with you privately?

No. If you’re interested in private coaching, business development and custom website design, you can apply directly for a Fast Track Call.

Do you only work with established bloggers?

No. I work with women starting from scratch and women who already have established businesses.

Your current revenue isn’t the only consideration when determining whether we’re a good fit for private support.

Do I need to know how to use artificial intelligence?

No advanced AI knowledge is required to begin exploring my business education. Individual courses may have specific technology or software requirements.

What if I’m not sure what I want to sell yet?

That’s a normal business development challenge.

Understanding your expertise, potential customers and offer opportunities is part of the business strategy I teach.

You don’t need to have every decision made before getting started.

You don’t need to do everything.

You need to understand what matters next.

The problem with trying to build a business through disconnected pieces of advice is that everything begins to feel equally important.

  • Your website.

  • Your content.
  • Your social media.
  • Your offers.
  • Your branding.
  • The newest AI tools.

But the right priorities depend on where you are and what your business needs. That’s why I created different experiences for different stages, challenges and ambitions.

Choose the support that makes sense for you. And if you’re ready for someone to personally help you understand what your business could become, I’d love to explore that with you.
