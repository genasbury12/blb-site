Part 01

Capture your expertise.

Before Claude can help you create a course, it needs to understand what you’re teaching and how you actually do the work.

Begin by documenting your expertise, identifying the result your course helps students achieve and capturing the processes behind your methodology.

Instead of asking AI to invent a curriculum from a broad subject, give it meaningful knowledge to work from. Build the education around real processes, real expertise and work you’ve actually done.

Part 02

Organize your knowledge and build your resource library.

Create an organized foundation for your course development process.

Explore how to set up Claude for your business, connect your course creation workflow to ClickUp and develop a reliable system for storing your documents, prompts, processes and resources.

Learn how to file new materials, manage versions, connect related resources and maintain an organized library as your course develops.

Your course becomes more than a collection of disconnected documents and conversations with AI. It becomes a structured educational project.

Part 03

Develop your curriculum.

Turn your documented expertise into an intentional learning experience.

Create your curriculum, develop your lessons, review what Claude produces and establish what students need to accomplish throughout the course.

Evaluate whether your lessons give students the knowledge, tools and instructions required to complete each stage. Identify missing lessons before they become problems for your customers.

You’re building a curriculum around implementation, not simply a list of topics you could teach.

Module 04

Make Your Prompts Work for Students

Your business is different from your students’ businesses. So why give every student a prompt written around your individual circumstances? Learn how to turn your own Claude prompts into reusable student prompts that incorporate each student’s information.

  • ✓Write your course rules. Establish the rules every student prompt should follow, including your writing preferences and standards for honest information.
  • ✓Turn your prompts into student prompts. Replace your individual business information with personalized Passport fields so the prompts can be used by different students.
  • ✓Give students templates, never a blank prompt. Turn the resources you’ve already developed into customizable templates with Make It Mine prompts that help students rebuild them around their own brands.

By the end of this module, you’ll have a system for developing student prompts and resources designed to adapt to individual circumstances.

Module 05

Build the Lesson Boards

This is where your educational content becomes an interactive learning experience. Learn how to develop the course environment your students will actually use.

  • ✓Build your first module board. Create a branded lesson board with organized educational content, implementation steps, prompts and tools.
  • ✓Add quick check quizzes. Develop lesson quizzes with instant feedback based on what students have just learned.
  • ✓Add missions, XP, badges and lesson locks. Create a gamified implementation experience that recognizes completed actions and guides students through the intended learning order.
  • ✓Save progress and the student Playbook. Explore how to preserve student progress and collect the work each student completes throughout the course.
  • ✓Build your course home. Create one organized starting point where students can access their individual module boards.
  • ✓Add the private student dashboard. Develop progress tracking, upcoming lesson recommendations, weekly priorities and a space to record accomplishments.
  • ✓Add a feedback board. Give students a place to communicate where they’re getting stuck.
  • ✓Share your course with students. Learn how Claude artifact sharing works and test your course from a student’s perspective.

This module takes you beyond designing an attractive course interface. It teaches you how to organize the individual elements of a functioning interactive learning experience.

Module 06

Make the Videos

What if you could create professionally organized educational lessons without sitting in front of a camera and manually recording every explanation? Learn my process for creating three different types of educational video content.

  • ✓Narrated motion slides in your own voice. Develop your lesson scripts with Claude, create branded motion slides and use an authorized voice clone through Fish Audio or ElevenLabs to narrate your education.
  • ✓Board tour videos. Create guided video tours that explain how students should navigate the interactive lesson boards and use the available resources.
  • ✓Watch Claude do it walkthroughs. Learn how to demonstrate your prompts and processes using a separate demonstration project, with private information protected.

Instead of relying exclusively on one type of educational video, learn how to develop instructional content that explains, demonstrates and supports the work your students need to complete.

Module 07

Update and Launch

Creating the course is only part of the process. You also need a system for maintaining it, giving students access and improving the experience based on their feedback.

  • ✓Push one fix to every board. Learn how to update shared course materials consistently across multiple module boards.
  • ✓Generate your Everything Inside list. Create an accurate inventory of your completed course directly from the live course files. No invented deliverables. No inflated numbers. No features you haven’t actually built.
  • ✓Write access and claim emails. Develop the welcome and access instructions your students need to begin their educational experience.
  • ✓Run a small beta and fix what sticks. Collect student feedback, identify the lessons causing the most confusion and prioritize meaningful improvements.

By the end of this stage, you’ll understand the processes behind preparing, testing, maintaining and launching your interactive educational product.

Is this just a course about using Claude to write lessons?

No.
