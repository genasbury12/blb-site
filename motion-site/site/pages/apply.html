The problem

You’ve been trying to figure out what to do next. But what if you’re asking the wrong questions altogether?

  • Maybe you’re wondering whether you’ve chosen the right niche.

  • Maybe you’ve been publishing content for months without making any meaningful income.
  • Maybe you have several business ideas but don’t know which one has the greatest potential.
