Frequently asked questions

Can I purchase individual courses?

Absolutely. Every available standalone course inside The BOSS Academy can be purchased individually. Choose the education that addresses your current business needs.

Can I purchase the entire course library?

Yes. The BOSS Academy All Access gives you access to the complete included collection instead of purchasing each course separately.

Do I need to complete the courses in a particular order?

No. The Academy is organized around specialized business skills, not a single curriculum. You can choose courses based on your interests, existing knowledge and current business priorities.

Are the courses suitable for beginners?

Yes. The Academy is designed for women at different stages of business. Individual course sales pages explain what each course covers and who it is best suited for.

Will new courses be added?

The BOSS Academy is designed to be an evolving educational collection, with new courses exploring modern business strategies, emerging technology and relevant industry developments.

Is The BOSS Academy the same as the Blogging Ladies Membership?

No. The Blogging Ladies Membership provides a structured business methodology through the eight module BOSS Business System and its implementation resources.

The BOSS Academy is a separate collection of specialized courses that explore individual business subjects in greater depth.

Is the entire Academy included in the $47 membership?

No. Academy courses and All Access are separate educational purchases.

Do Academy courses include private coaching?

Academy courses are designed for independent learning. If you want personalized business strategy, custom website design and done for you implementation, explore the BOSS Business Experience.
