Genasys

From my aunt’s basement to a money making business

I want your blog to make money. Not just keep you busy.

Hi, I’m Genasys, founder of Boss Lady Bloggers.

I built this business in the middle of real life, when there were more responsibilities than uninterrupted hours…

I built a money making business from my aunt’s basement during nap times as a single mom.

I know what it means to build a business in the middle of real life. What I don’t believe is that women should spend years creating content with no idea how that work leads to a sale.

THE BIG IDEA

I teach blogging differently because a blog is not the business.

Your blog helps people find you. Your business gives them a reason to buy. That means you need to know who you’re helping, what they’re struggling with, what you offer, how you communicate its value, and how someone becomes a customer.

I help women connect those pieces through offer strategy, sales psychology, copywriting, branding, website design, and practical implementation.

So, with that being said… let me show you what makes me a “gloriously” imperfect human.

because, spoiler alert: I’m just like you

Hey, I’m Genasys!

I’m a chocolate-obsessed boy mom who struggles hard with constantly changing my mind and pivoting. (Shoutout to my fellow overthinkers!)

I love food. Like, it’s a real problem. And if I could plan my day around delicious chocolate lava cake, I absolutely would.

My son is 4 now, and we live in a house in Ohio. He’s in school, and I work full time on this business. You’re not going to find me sipping cocktails on a beach with my laptop anytime soon. I mean, I have done that before, but let’s be honest, that’s just not my current life.

Most days, he’s at school while I’m here building Boss Lady Bloggers. The second he’s home, I’m back to chasing him, keeping him occupied, and stopping him from hurting himself. (Because, you know. Boys.) And when we both need a day off? We head to Kings Island and ride rollercoasters.

another fun fact

I have no friends. Okay, maybe my mom counts, and my clients are kind of my besties by default. But I’m rooting for myself to find a sweet old-lady friend one day. You know, the kind where we take knitting classes together and talk about the weather like it’s fascinating.

So, yeah, that’s me. Real, imperfect, and figuring it all out one day (and one cake) at a time.

Alrighty then, let’s move on. Because while I’m an open book, this page isn’t just about me. It’s about how I can help you.

A “FIGURED-IT-OUT-THE-HARD-WAY” MOMENT

I’m qualified, I swear.

Because building a blog, brand, and business from scratch while juggling real life? Yeah, I’ve been there, and now it’s what I help others do every day. Through all the messy pivots, late-night brainstorms, and moments of how the heck do I do this?, I’ve learned what works (and what doesn’t) when it comes to building a blog and turning it into a real, money-making business.

it started in 7th grade

My love for writing didn’t start here. It started way back in 7th grade. I was the kid who couldn’t get enough of journaling, crafting short stories for class assignments, and filling up my very own book of poems. Writing was my thing. It made me feel creative, alive, and like I had something worth saying.

That passion for storytelling is exactly what brought me here today.

let’s start from the beginning

In 2017, I started my very first blog on an old 2010 MacBook, sitting in the basement apartment I was renting from my aunt. I didn’t have a job, and I definitely didn’t have money. What I did have, though, was determination. I was desperate to figure out how to make money from home, and I spent hours, days, researching every possible option. That’s when I stumbled upon blogging.

It felt like the perfect fit. I’ve always loved writing, and the idea of turning that passion into income? Sign me up.

But, let me tell you, it was not a walk in the park.

I spent two whole years working on that first blog, pouring my heart into it, only to end up with nothing to show for it financially. Why? Because I put all my eggs into one basket, affiliate marketing and ads, and neither of those worked for me. Even with a decent amount of traffic coming to my blog every month, I wasn’t seeing the results I wanted.

After two years of spinning my wheels, I’d had enough. I realized I couldn’t keep winging it, hoping things would magically fall into place. So, I made a decision: I was going to invest in learning about business. And what I learned completely changed my perspective.

Business > Side Hustle Blog.

If you want your blog to actually make money, you have to get serious about it. Treat it like a real business, not a hobby. That means creating your own offers instead of relying on affiliate marketing, and learning how to have others be affiliates for you.

That mindset shift? It changed everything.

Fast forward to now, and I have a money making business doing exactly what I love: teaching others how to properly monetize their blogs and avoid the mistakes I made. Along the way, I also learned the importance of branding and website design. A strong brand and a professional website aren’t just nice extras. They’re necessary. They help you stand out, build trust, and attract the right audience.

Now, I get to combine everything I’ve learned, monetization strategies, branding, website design, and blog coaching, to help others turn their blogs into businesses they love. And I can’t wait to help you do the same.

GOOD FOR THE PLOT

From blog to business: my plot twist

Starting a blog and building a business has to be one of the most ridiculously nerve-wracking, overwhelming, what-if-I-mess-this-up decisions ever. So, naturally, I did what I always do: jumped in headfirst without a perfect plan and figured it out along the way. That’s how all my best decisions have been made. Others include:

Starting my first blog on a 2010 MacBook with no idea what I was doing Deciding to leave a relationship of eight years Moving into my own place with a toddler in his terrible 2s Attempting to potty train a toddler while working from home

Looking back? Every one of those moments wasn’t just a leap. It was a plot twist that brought me exactly where I’m supposed to be. And if you’re ready to take your leap, I’m here to help you make it happen.

Now, why do you need me?

I probably should have started with this, right? Oh well, better late than never.

You have big dreams for your blog. Maybe you want to grow an engaged audience, turn readers into buyers, and finally make money doing something you love. Buuuut… you’re not exactly sure where to start. And the whole “blogging as a business” thing? It feels overwhelming, like trying to put together IKEA furniture without the instructions.

You’ve got ideas, but they’re all over the place. You think you know who your audience is, but figuring out how to actually get their attention (and keep it)? That’s a whole other story. Meanwhile, your to-do list is growing, your website is unfinished, and the “monetize my blog” dream feels more like a nice fantasy than a real plan.

Sound familiar?

Here’s where I come in. I’m here to help you turn your blog into a thriving, money-making business, with a brand that makes an impact, a website that works for you, and a clear plan to create offers your audience can’t wait to buy.

And when I say I’ll help you do it right, I mean backed by strategy, research, and everything I’ve learned about blogging, branding, and business. No fluff, no guessing, just actionable steps that get you results.

Pretty matters. But pretty alone does not pay you.

I love a beautiful website. I’m a Showit designer, after all. But a gorgeous homepage that doesn’t tell people what to do next is still a missed opportunity.

The same goes for content. A blog post can be useful, polished, and perfectly optimized for search, yet still have no connection to a product or service.

I teach you to think about the sale from the beginning, then use your blog and website to make that sale easier.
