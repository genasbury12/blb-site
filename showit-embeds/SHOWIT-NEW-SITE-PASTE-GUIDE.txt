HOW TO PASTE (each page)
------------------------
1. In Showit, open or create the page with the slug above.
2. Delete everything on the page canvas.
3. Add ONE Embed Code canvas, stretch it full width and full height.
4. Paste the entire contents of the matching file.
5. Set the canvas height roughly to the page length (you can adjust
   after previewing; the embed sizes itself).
6. Publish.

IMPORTANT: if any of your Showit pages use a different slug than the
list above, tell Claude the real slug and you'll get updated files
(the menus on every page link to these exact slugs).

CONTACT FORM
------------
Works out of the box. It sends through FormSubmit to
hello@bossladybloggers.com (same service as your old site) and then
shows your Thank You page. THE FIRST TIME someone submits (make it
yourself!), FormSubmit emails you a confirmation link - click it once
and the form is permanently active.

WAITLIST FORMS (group + course)
-------------------------------
Right now they show the pretty "You're on the list!" message but do not
save the email (Showit pages cannot talk to Flodesk directly).
To make them real: in Flodesk create an INLINE form for each waitlist
(Forms > New form > Inline), copy each form's embed code, and send both
codes to Claude - you'll get updated GroupWaitlist/CourseWaitlist files
with the real Flodesk forms styled into the design.

IMAGES
------
All photos, logos and artwork load from a free image host that is
already set up and verified working. Nothing for you to upload or
manage. (Technical note: it's the Netlify project - leave it alone,
it now quietly serves your site's images. Deleting it breaks every
image on the site.)

PREVIEW QUIRK
-------------
In the Showit EDITOR the animations may look frozen or sections may sit
there unanimated - that's just the editor. There is also a built-in
safety so nothing can ever stay invisible. Always judge by the
published page, not the editor canvas.
