HOW TO PASTE (each page)
------------------------
1. In Showit, open or create the page with the slug above.
2. Delete everything on the page canvas.
3. Add ONE Embed Code canvas, stretch it full width and full height.
4. Paste the entire contents of the matching file.
5. Set the canvas height roughly to the page length (you can adjust
   after previewing; the embed sizes itself).
6. Publish.

IMPORTANT: if any of your Showit pages use a different slug than the
list above, tell Claude the real slug and you'll get updated files
(the menus on every page link to these exact slugs).

BLOG POST TEMPLATE (the page every blog post uses)
--------------------------------------------------
Your blog posts live in WordPress, so the post page itself is a Showit
BLOG TEMPLATE, not a regular page. On that template, Showit's own blog
widgets (post title, featured image, post content) pull in each article
automatically - you cannot replace those with an embed. But you CAN
place embed canvases around them. That is exactly how the designed
post page gets rebuilt:

1. STYLE THE WIDGETS (15 min, once). Open Blog > your post template in
   Showit and set the fonts/colors from the style spec file you already
   have (SHOWIT-BLOG-TEMPLATE-STYLE-SPEC.txt). That makes the title,
   featured image and article body match the new brand.

2. PLACE THESE 7 EMBEDS on the same template page, top to bottom:

   File                   What it is              Where / canvas size
   PostNavBar.html        Site navigation bar      Very top, full width,
                          (logo + links + BOSS)    ~110px tall (taller on
                                                   mobile so links wrap)
   PostHero.html          Featured image band.     Under the nav, above
                          Pulls each post's own    the post title, full
                          image + category chip    content width, ~420px
                          from WordPress
   (then Showit's own post title, meta and content widgets)
   PostAuthorCard.html    "Meet Genasys" card      Sidebar, next to the
                          with your photo          post content, ~340x320
   PostDownloadCard.html  Foundations Builder      Sidebar, under the
                          free download card       author card, ~340x220
   PostNextStepBand.html  Dark plum CTA band       Under the post
                                                   content, ~140px tall
   PostRelatedBand.html   "Keep reading" band      Near the bottom, full
                          with 3 recent posts      width, ~560px tall
   PostFooter.html        The FULL site footer:    Very bottom, full
                          your bio, Get around     width, ~1000px tall
                          links, live Instagram    desktop / ~1700px
                          feed, berry marquee      mobile

   The nav bar and footer make every blog post feel like the rest of
   the site, so readers can get anywhere from a post.

3. THE KEEP-READING BAND IS AUTOMATIC. It pulls your 3 newest posts
   (picture + title + link) straight from your WordPress blog every
   time the page loads, and skips the post the reader is already on.
   Nothing to update, ever.

4. ONE LINE TO EDIT PER POST (optional). The next-step band has a
   placeholder line: "[Next step: the free training or offer this post
   points to]". Showit uses ONE template for all posts, so either
   replace that line once with something evergreen that fits every
   post, or skip that band entirely. The button already links to your
   free trainings page.

Because it is a template, you do all of this ONCE and every blog post
(old and new) instantly wears the new design.

CONTACT FORM
------------
Works out of the box. It sends through FormSubmit to
hello@bossladybloggers.com (same service as your old site) and then
shows your Thank You page. THE FIRST TIME someone submits (make it
yourself!), FormSubmit emails you a confirmation link - click it once
and the form is permanently active.

WAITLIST FORMS (group + course)
-------------------------------
Right now they show the pretty "You're on the list!" message but do not
save the email (Showit pages cannot talk to Flodesk directly).
To make them real: in Flodesk create an INLINE form for each waitlist
(Forms > New form > Inline), copy each form's embed code, and send both
codes to Claude - you'll get updated GroupWaitlist/CourseWaitlist files
with the real Flodesk forms styled into the design.

IMAGES
------
All photos, logos and artwork load from a free image host that is
already set up and verified working. Nothing for you to upload or
manage. (Technical note: it's the Netlify project - leave it alone,
it now quietly serves your site's images. Deleting it breaks every
image on the site.)

PREVIEW QUIRK
-------------
In the Showit EDITOR the animations may look frozen or sections may sit
there unanimated - that's just the editor. There is also a built-in
safety so nothing can ever stay invisible. Always judge by the
published page, not the editor canvas.
