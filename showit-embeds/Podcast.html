I’m Genasys, and we’re talking about what it really takes to turn a blog into a business.

Offers, marketing, sales, mistakes, strategies, and systems. No pretending that every blogging strategy works for everyone. No telling you to publish 100 posts and hope. Just honest conversations and practical advice.

If you’ve ever thought, “I have no idea what I’m doing wrong,” this podcast is for you.

Maybe you’ve been blogging for six months or three years. Maybe you’ve made a little from affiliate links or ads. Or maybe you created a product and can’t figure out why nobody bought it.

Hi, I’m Genasys.

I’m the founder of Boss Lady Bloggers, a blog coach and website designer who believes your blog should do more than collect pageviews.

I built a money making business from my aunt’s basement while raising my son as a single mom. One of the biggest lessons I’ve learned is that your blog is not the business. It’s one way people discover the business.

That’s the perspective I bring to every episode, along with the honest parts of entrepreneurship that don’t always make it into polished Instagram posts.
