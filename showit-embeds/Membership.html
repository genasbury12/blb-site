SEASON 1, EPISODE 1: THE FIRST TIME YOU HIT PUBLISH

Remember the first time you hit publish?

Maybe it was a recipe. Maybe it was your birth story, or your DIY dresser makeover, or the day you finally said the thing out loud. You picked a blog name you loved. You spent way too long choosing fonts. You refreshed the page just to see if anyone had commented.

If any of these make you smile, you’re one of us:

Those were the days. You were a writer before you ever called yourself a blogger. And that girl? She’s still in there.

For a lot of us, blogging was more than a hobby. One woman told me blogging is like therapy. Another called it a journal to your life.

And somewhere along the way, it stopped feeling like that.

Somewhere between the algorithm and AI, you lost the joy.

Everyone started telling you blogging was dead. Post Reels instead. Go viral on TikTok. Let AI write it for you. Chase traffic, add affiliate links, wait for the money.

Blogging went from pouring your heart out on MySpace to algorithm panic. From writing what you felt to “Do I need to dance on camera now?”

✦ Breaking news ✦

Here’s the truth: blogging isn’t dead. It’s having a comeback.

Low-rise jeans: came back ✓ Butterfly clips: came back ✓ Y2K everything: came back ✓ Blogging: it’s next ✦

Here’s what nobody is saying out loud. When everything online is written by AI, what becomes rare?

The human.

AI can write 1,000 posts about potty training. It has never potty trained a toddler at 6 AM with cold coffee in one hand. It can list budget meals. It has never fed a family on a tight grocery budget. It can describe grief, homeschooling, burnout or a kitchen makeover. It has never lived a single one of them.

That’s why human content is about to be worth more, not less. When readers can’t tell what’s real anymore, they go looking for someone they trust. A real face. A real story. A real woman who has actually done the thing.

That’s you.

And your blog is still the one place online you own. Social platforms change the rules overnight. Search keeps changing. But your blog, your email list and your offer are yours. That’s not a dying hobby. That’s a business no algorithm can take away.

Here’s what most blogging advice got backwards:

“I need more traffic before I can make money.”

You need something to sell first. Then every reader has somewhere to go.

“Blogs make money from ads and affiliates.”

They pay pennies until you’re huge. Your own offer can pay you now, even with a small audience.

“AI can write my blog for me.”

AI can’t tell your story, so your voice stays yours. But AI should absolutely do the heavy lifting on the tech and the busywork, and I’ll show you exactly how.

“I need to figure it all out alone.”

Nope. That’s exactly what the Blogging Ladies are for.

Dear diary, a true story

I learned this the hard way. I started my lifestyle blog in 2017, in my aunt’s basement, on a 2010 MacBook. Two years in, I’d made about $50 in affiliate money and never got paid, because the cash-out minimum was $100.

When I built my own offer first, my first Stripe payout was $18.52. Within 3 to 4 months, I’d made about $4,000.

Same girl. Same laptop. Same love of blogging. I just finally had the right order, and people in my corner.
