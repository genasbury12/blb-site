My six part process for turning a blog into a business that sells

01

Find your money opportunity

First, we figure out what people should pay you for. If you don’t have an offer, we’ll explore your audience’s problems, expertise, and opportunities. If you already have something to sell, we’ll examine why it may not be producing the results you want.

Your outcome: A clear offer strategy and evidence to test.

02

Build your front end sales process

Now we make sure people who discover your blog can discover what you sell. How visitors find you, how content connects to offers, where calls to action belong, and what your sales page needs to communicate.

Your outcome: A defined path connecting content, website, and paid offers.

03

Build your back end sales process

Not everyone buys the first time they visit. We’ll map how your free resources, email list, welcome messages, relevant offers, and follow ups continue the conversation.

Your outcome: An email sales strategy for nurturing interested people and making relevant offers.

04

Prelaunch your offer

Stop creating products in secret and hoping people buy when you finally announce them. We’ll research the market, test interest, plan prelaunch content and emails, and prepare the buying experience.

Your outcome: A tailored prelaunch campaign plan.

05

Launch and sell

We’ll plan the announcement, launch emails, relevant social content, objection handling, and follow ups. When ready, you’ll put the campaign into action and collect real customer feedback.

Your outcome: A focused campaign or offer test and initial evidence about buyer response.

06

Improve and repeat

We’ll review what happened. What created interest? Where did prospects stop? What sold? What should you adjust?

Your outcome: A clearer process and a prioritized next 90 day plan.
