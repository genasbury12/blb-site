Four steps, about fifteen minutes

Member home ◆ Quick Start

Right then, friend

Start here. Then close the tab.

This is the whole beginning. Four steps, about fifteen minutes, and you will have done the thing that most people never get around to doing.

Two pages to print One five minute video One decision Two small things to lock it in

What you do not need: a spreadsheet, a perfect month to start in, a spare hour, or anyone else in the house to be on board yet. You need a pen and the next fifteen minutes.

1

Print these two pages.

About three minutes

Print two copies of each while you are at it. One for this payday, one for the next, and you will not have to think about it again for a month.

The Payday Reset Page

1 page ◆ PDF ◆ print two

Every dollar of this paycheck gets a job before it goes anywhere. This is the page the whole membership is built around.

Open and print

The One-Page Monthly Budget

2 pages ◆ PDF ◆ planned against actual

The month on one sheet, with a column for what you planned and a column for what actually happened. No judgment in either column.

Open and print

No printer? That is not a problem.

Both files are fillable, so you can type into them on a phone or laptop. Or do what I did for two years and draw the four columns in a cheap notebook. The page is a prompt, not a rule.

Or skip ahead and come back to printing later.

3

Pick the one thing you are working on first.

One minute, and it is the important one

One. Not all seven. Everything in the vault will still be there next month, and trying to fix all of it in one week is exactly how this falls apart. Which of these sounds most like your house right now?

Cannot decide? Pick the first one. It is the right answer more often than not, and you can change it any time.

    Week two

    Add the meal plan

    One Sunday, one page, dinner decided from what is already in the pantry.

    Week three

    Week four

    Come to the live session

    Bring what worked and what did not. We plan next month together from there.

    Four weeks in, payday is a routine you barely think about. That is the whole promise, and it is a smaller one than most people make.

    and one more thing

    When a week goes sideways.

    It will. A kid gets sick, the car makes a noise, and the page sits on the counter untouched for eleven days. I need you to hear the next part properly, because it is the reason most people quit and it does not have to be.

    You have not failed and you are not behind. There is no group to catch up to, no lessons expiring, and nothing here keeping score. Come back, print a fresh page, and start with the next payday. That is not a consolation prize. That is genuinely how this works, and it is how I did it too.

    progress over perfection, always ◆ Brooke
