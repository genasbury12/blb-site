August release

The back to school reset

School supply season is the month that quietly wrecks a good budget, so this one lands before it does instead of after. Two pages and a short training, and you will have the whole of August planned before the lists come home.

Added 1 August 3 pieces About 25 minutes to work through Yours to keep, forever
if you only do one thing

Print the School Supply Plan and fill in last year’s numbers.

Not this year’s. Last year’s, from memory or the bank app, because that is the number that tells you what August actually costs your family. Everything else in this release gets easier once that figure is on paper, and it takes about ten minutes.
