Identity Mark

Logo & Lockups

The Purposeful Pennies logo is a clean typographic wordmark. Georgia bold in deep sage, preceded by a single coral dot, with the tagline set in Caveat script beneath. No illustration, no icon, no ornament. The simplicity is the point: it reads instantly at any size, works on every background in the palette, and never competes with the warmth of the photography or the copy.

Logo Do's

Use on cream, light sage, coral blush, deep sage, or white
Keep clear space equal to the cap height on all sides
Use the reversed (cream) version on deep sage
Scale proportionally, keeping the dot aligned to the baseline
Drop the tagline when the logo appears below 16px

Logo Don'ts

Do not substitute another typeface for Georgia
Do not recolor the dot anything other than coral
Do not add an icon, coin, or illustration to the lockup
Do not place on busy photographic backgrounds
Do not stretch, outline, or add drop shadows to the type

Photography Direction

Imagery Guidelines

☼
Hero Portrait
Brooke ◆ Kitchen, Morning Light

Warm smile, natural not posed. Mug in hand, real light. Maybe kids' art on the fridge. "Friend who has got you" energy.

✎
Detail Shot
Hands Writing in a Budget Notebook

Overhead, warm. Worn notebook, coffee mug, candle or flowers in soft focus. Real money work with warmth.

❤
Connection
Two Friends Over Coffee

Two women's hands around warm mugs at a kitchen table. Real friendship, not stock photography posing.

✕
Never Use
Stock Photo Perfection

No pristine kitchens, no overly posed families, no aspirational "rich life" imagery. No white marble.

✕
Never Use
Shame-Adjacent Imagery

No "stressed woman at a desk with bills." We show the before only in words. Visuals always carry hope.

✕
Never Use
Cold or Clinical Tones

No blue-grey or corporate photography. No spreadsheets as heroes. No calculators or fanned cash.

Color Temperature

Warm. Always warm. Golden hour light, morning glow, candle warmth. Never cool blues or harsh whites.

Emotion in Frame

Hope, warmth, relief, quiet joy. Messy-real beats styled-perfect. Kids in frame are always a plus.

Setting

Kitchen table, living room, backyard. Brooke's actual world. Not a studio. Not a conference room.
