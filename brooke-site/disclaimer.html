01Not professional advice

The content on Purposeful Pennies, including blog posts, emails, printables, worksheets, trackers, courses, membership resources, and community conversation, is educational and informational personal finance content based on personal experience.

It is not financial, investment, legal, tax, accounting, or other professional advice, and it is not tailored to your situation. Nothing here creates a professional or advisory relationship between you and Purposeful Pennies.

02Not a licensed professional

Brooke Michelle Watrous is not a licensed financial advisor, certified financial planner, CPA, accountant, attorney, or credentialed financial professional of any kind. She holds no licence, registration, or certification in financial services.

Before you make any significant financial decision, please talk to a licensed professional who can look at your actual numbers. That includes decisions about debt, credit, taxes, insurance, investing, retirement, and anything that touches your family's long term security. A good advisor is worth the appointment.

Purposeful Pennies covers budgeting, debt payoff, saving, and frugal living for families managing on one income.

They are not a promise, a projection, or a typical result. Individual results vary enormously and depend on your income, your expenses, your debt, your family, your health, your local economy, your discipline, and a hundred things neither of us controls.
