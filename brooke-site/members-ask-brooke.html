What makes a question I can really answer

How do I save more money? We have about 60 left at the end of most months. Should that go to the car fund or the credit card first?
My grocery budget is not working. I set 150 a week for five of us and I keep landing at 190. Where do people usually find that 40?
I keep falling off the budget. I do the payday page fine, then stop by week two when the spending log gets behind. Is there a version that survives that?

See the pattern? Numbers and a specific fork in the road. That is what turns a nice reply into one you can act on tonight.

Specific to your numbers, with what I would actually do. If I do not know, I will say that too.

What I cannot help with, and why.

I would rather tell you now than leave you waiting on an answer that was never coming.

  • Which shares, funds, or retirement accounts to put money into
  • Tax questions, or anything that turns on your specific filing situation
  • Bankruptcy, debt settlement, or negotiating with a creditor on your behalf
  • Credit repair, or anything involving your actual credit file
  • Whether you can afford a mortgage on a particular house

Not because those questions are silly. Because they are exactly the questions where wrong advice from a well meaning stranger does real damage, and I am a mom who got out of debt, not a licensed adviser. For any of those, a fee only financial planner, an accountant, or a nonprofit credit counsellor is the right call, and I am happy to help you work out which one you need.

Billing, login trouble, or anything about your membership goes to the contact page instead, where I will see it faster.
