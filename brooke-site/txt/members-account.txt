<!--
  ============================================================
  PURPOSEFUL PENNIES  ·  YOUR ACCOUNT   /members-account
  Showit + MemberSpace
  ============================================================

  WHAT THIS PAGE IS, AND IS NOT
  MemberSpace already owns the real account controls: card on file,
  plan, renewal date, cancel button, password. Rebuilding those here
  is not possible and would be a lie if attempted.

  So this page does the two things MemberSpace cannot:
     1. Sends her to the right control with one obvious button
     2. States the promises in Brooke's words, in plain language,
        where she will look for them

  ------------------------------------------------------------
  THE RULE THAT MATTERS MOST ON THIS PAGE
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  NO INVENTED ACCOUNT DATA. This is a static Showit page. It cannot
  know her renewal date, her card, or what she paid. Printing a
  plausible looking "next payment 11 September" would be a fabricated
  number on a billing page, which is the worst place in the whole
  build to put one.

  Anything specific therefore says so and links to MemberSpace.
  data-ms-member-info="firstName" is the only member value used, and
  it is one MemberSpace genuinely fills in. If you later confirm other
  data attributes work on your plan, they can be added, but check them
  on a real logged in account first.

  ------------------------------------------------------------
  NO DARK PATTERNS, DELIBERATELY
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  Four pages already promise cancelling takes two clicks with nobody
  trying to talk her out of it. So the cancel section is a plain
  button and a clear explanation, with no retention offer in front of
  it, no survey, and no guilt. If that promise is ever softened here,
  it contradicts the sales page, the FAQ, the thank you page, and the
  Circle. Leave it alone.

  ------------------------------------------------------------
  MEMBERSPACE
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  Protection inherited from the /members-* prefix rule.
  Every ms-link needs the MemberSpace Link pasted in. There are more
  of them on this page than anywhere else, which is the point.
  The Showit override armour block is included. Do not remove it.

  FORMATTING RULE. Zero em dashes, zero en dashes. Separator is the
  diamond glyph.
  ============================================================
