The Monthly Money Reset

Working out your local time…

Forty five minutes where we plan the month together, out loud, in real numbers. We set the grocery number, check the sinking funds, and work through whatever you bring. This is the thing that keeps the rest of it going.

The room opens ten minutes early if you like arriving before things start. It runs on the second Tuesday of every month.

No surprises

What actually happens in the forty five minutes.

Written out because knowing the shape of a thing makes it much easier to walk into.

First 5

Arriving

Hellos, and I say who is new so nobody feels like they wandered into something already in progress. No introductions required from you.

Next 15

We plan the month

I share my screen and fill in the page for the month ahead with my own real numbers. You fill in yours alongside me. This is the core of it.

Next 15

Questions from the room

You type them in the chat or unmute, whichever you prefer. I take them in the order they land. If it is a question one person has, it is usually a question six people have.

Last 10

What is coming, and one thing each

What is landing in the vault next month, then everybody names one thing they are doing before the next session. Saying it out loud is most of why it gets done.

Two minutes of prep

Bring three things, if you can.

one

Your numbers

What is coming in this month and roughly what is going out. Rough is fine. Nobody is auditing you.

two

A printed page

The payday page or the monthly budget, whichever you are working from. Pen beats typing while you are watching a screen.

three

One question

The thing you have been going round in circles on. Written down, so you actually ask it instead of talking yourself out of it.

And if you turn up with none of that, still come. Plenty of women join with a cup of tea and no page at all, listen to somebody else’s question, and leave with the thing they needed. Turning up beats preparing.

Before you worry about it

Things people ask before their first one.

Cameras are optional, always. Plenty of people keep theirs off the whole time and it is genuinely not a thing.
Background noise is fine. Children, dogs, a dishwasher. We are all at home and it is eight in the evening.
Nobody gets called on. You will never be put on the spot. If you want to speak, you say so.
You can arrive late or leave early. No apology needed and nobody will remark on it.
Chat questions are equally welcome. Most questions come through the chat, not out loud.
Your numbers stay in the room. Same rule as the Circle. What is shared here stays here.

Every replay

nothing here yet, and that is fine

The first replay lands after the first session.

Every session is recorded and shows up here the morning after, with the notes and any pages we used. Come to the live one if you can, and if bedtime wins, it will be waiting for you.
