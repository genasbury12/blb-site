The Circle

A private room of moms making one income work. No comparison, no judgment, and nobody pretending their month went perfectly. Come in, say hello, and lurk as long as you like.

Open the Circle Help me write my first post

The room lives in a private Facebook group, so nothing you post there shows up on your own page or in anyone’s feed but ours. Not on Facebook?

What this room is for

  • Saying the number out loud when you cannot say it to anyone else.
  • Asking before you spend, when you want a second opinion at nine at night.
  • Small wins. Genuinely small ones. Under budget by four dollars counts.
  • What is for dinner, from people cooking on the same kind of budget.
  • Finding out you are not the only one, which is most of why it works.

What it is not

  • A place anyone will judge your numbers. That is the whole point of a private room.
  • A performance. Nobody here has it together, including me.
  • Somewhere you have to post to belong. Reading quietly is allowed forever.
  • A marketplace. No selling, no recruiting, no affiliate links.
  • Professional financial advice, mine included.

The hardest post is the first one

Here is exactly what to say.

Copy this, fill in the four blanks, post it in Welcome and introductions. It takes about ninety seconds and it is the single thing that most predicts whether somebody is still here in six months.

You do not have to be clever, and you absolutely do not have to explain how you got here. Four facts is plenty.

If the thought of posting makes you feel a bit sick

That is normal and you are not the only one reading this thinking it. Nobody in that room is scoring your introduction, and every single one of them was exactly this nervous. If it still feels like too much today, read for a week first. The door does not close.

Your first post

Hi, I’m [your name] and we are in [your state]. We feed [how many] on one income. The one thing I want to be different by next payday is [the thing]. Right now I am working on [which system]. Glad to be here.
Then paste it straight into the group.
The house rules

Seven things that keep this room safe.

Short, and I do enforce them. A room where women share real numbers only works if it is genuinely safe, and that does not happen by accident.

one

No judgment about numbers. Ever.

Not about the debt, not about the income, not about the thing she bought. Everyone here started somewhere and nobody knows the full story.

two

What is shared here stays here.

No screenshots, no repeating somebody’s situation outside the room, not even without a name attached.

three

No selling, recruiting, or affiliate links.

This room is full of women being honest about money, which makes it a target. It is not a customer list and I will remove anyone treating it like one.

four

Offer advice, do not prescribe it.

“Here is what worked for us” lands very differently from “you should”. Her house, her call, always.

five

Food is food here.

We talk about meals as meals and budgets as budgets. No diet talk, no calorie counting, no comments about anybody’s body.

six

Nobody here is a financial adviser.

Me included. This is women sharing what worked in their own homes. For anything with legal or tax weight to it, please talk to a professional.

seven

Lurking is allowed forever.

Plenty of members never post and get their money’s worth from reading. You are not doing it wrong.

One more thing worth saying plainly. If somebody in that room makes you feel small, tell me and I will deal with it. I would rather lose a member than let this become another place where women feel bad about money.

Not on Facebook, and not planning to be?

Then this part is honest rather than tidy. The Circle is on Facebook for now because that is where this group of women already are every day, and a community nobody visits is not a community. But if you are not on there, you should not be quietly missing a piece of what you pay for.

So, until the room moves somewhere better:

  • Ask Brooke works entirely without Facebook. Post there and you get the same answer from me.
  • The monthly live session is the community bit in real time, and it is on Zoom, not Facebook.
  • Email me and I will pass a question into the room for you and bring the answers back.

And if enough of you tell me this is a problem, that moves the room. Founding members vote on what gets built, and this is exactly the sort of thing that vote is for.
