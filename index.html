06The part that changes everything

It really comes down to two things.

A blog that pays you isn't about posting more or going viral. It's two pieces working together: something to sell, and a way to lead people to it. Nail those two and everything else gets easier.

01

Something to sell

A blog with nothing to sell is a diary. Lovely, but it doesn't pay you. So we give you something of your own. A template, a guide, a workbook, a mini course, a service. Not someone else's product for a 3% cut. Yours, and you keep every dollar.

And if “selling” makes you cringe, breathe. You're not tricking anyone. You're saying “I made this thing that can help you” to someone already looking for it. When you believe in the offer, it stops feeling gross and starts feeling like service.

02

16Before you ask

I already know what you're thinking.

01Isn't it too late? Everyone says blogging is dead and AI does it all now.+
Blogging as “post a lot, get Google traffic, collect ad money” is dead. Good riddance. A blog that sells something of your own to a small list of the right readers is doing better than ever, because everyone else is busy churning out the same AI posts. AI made starting a blog easy. It didn't make anyone's blog pay. That part is still strategy, and that's what we do.
02Can't I just use AI for all this?+
For the writing, mostly yes, and we will. AI can't pick your offer, tell you your price is wrong, build your Showit site, or notice when you've gone quiet for three weeks. That's what you're paying for. The answers are free now. Doing it is still the hard part.
03I'm not techy. Like, at all.+
Good, because I build it. Showit is drag and drop, and you get two simple manuals and a live walkthrough when I hand you the keys. If you can use Canva, you can run your site.
04I'm just starting out. Do I need a big audience already?+
No. Every client I've had started with a small list or none. You need one thing to sell and the right 100 readers, not 100,000 strangers. We build the list on purpose, from the freebie up.
05I barely have time. Can I even do this?+
About 2 to 3 hours a week. One call a month, one thing to do between calls. I built it this way because it's how I have to work too, around a job and my son. Fall behind a week? You pick up where you left off.
06Do I need my niche figured out first?+
No. That's phase one. We figure out your purpose, your person, and your offer together before I touch a single design.
07What platform do you build on and why?+
Showit, with WordPress underneath for your posts. Fully custom, no code, and you own everything. Your domain, your content, your blog. It all comes with you.
