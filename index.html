Before you ask

I already know what you're thinking.

Isn't it too late to start a blog? Everyone says blogging is dead. +
Nope. People say that every single year while bloggers quietly build real businesses behind the scenes. What's actually dead is posting into the void with no plan. With the right foundation, starting now is more than fine. It's smart.
I'm not techy. Like, at all. +
Perfect, that's literally my job. I build your website for you, and it's drag and drop, so once it's done you can update it without losing your mind. No code, ever. Promise.
I'm just starting out. Do I need a big audience already? +
Not even a little. We start exactly where you are, even if that's zero followers and zero subscribers. The whole point is building the foundation that helps the right people actually find you.
I barely have time. Can I even do this? +
This is built for women with full, loud, busy lives. We work at a real human pace, and I make the decisions with you so you're not staring at a blank screen at midnight wondering what's next.
Do I need my niche figured out first? +
Nope. Figuring out your purpose, your person, and what you're selling is literally step one of the coaching. If you had it all figured out already, you wouldn't need a blog coach, would you?
What platform do you build on and why? +
I design fully custom websites on a drag-and-drop platform built for women who want gorgeous sites without ever touching code. It's flexible, it's beautiful, and it makes your blog easy to run once it's live.
