## The Business

- **Blog name:** Marriage & Retirement Abroad ("MRA")
- **Domain (planned, not purchased yet):** marriageandretirementabroad.com
- **One-liner:** Helping couples 45+ who dream of retiring in France start and complete the process with ease and confidence.
- **Niche angle:** retirement LIFESTYLE (not finances). Most retirement content is about saving; MRA is about what you retire TO.
- **Positioning title:** French Retirement Lifestyle Coach — "the person who makes it fun and easy for couples to retire in France."
- **Unique story:** Lisette lived in France for years and speaks fluent French. She and her husband (mid-50s, 4 adult kids between them) are planning their own retirement in the South of France.
- **Signature concept:** "retirement segments" — take 2–3 year segments of retirement abroad sooner, instead of waiting for one lump-sum retirement at the end of a career.

## Audience

- Married couples 45+ dreaming of retiring in France, intimidated by logistics, immigration law, and the language.
- They tried to DIY it and got more confused. They want someone to simplify and organize it with them.
- Their actual language (use in copy/FAQ/SEO):
  - "Is there a retirement visa for Americans who want to retire in France?"
  - "What are the best retirement places in France?"
  - "How do French people treat Americans who retire in their country?"
  - "How long does it take to learn to speak French fluently?" / "Is French easy to learn?"
  - "How much do you need to retire comfortably in France?"
- NOT for: people who hate travel, don't care about France, aren't near retirement.
- Dream traffic source: Google. Interested platforms: podcast (co-hosted with husband) first, YouTube later.
