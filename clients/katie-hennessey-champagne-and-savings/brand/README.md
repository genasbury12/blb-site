# Champagne & Savings Brand Identity

Draft v1, November 2026. Built from Genasys's chosen color direction and fashion illustration inspiration.

The idea: a champagne coupe (champagne) tied with a bow (fashion), with gold bubbles that read as sparkle and savings. It's elegant enough to feel "champagne taste," and soft and approachable enough for a millennial mom.

## Fonts

All free under the SIL Open Font License (Google Fonts), so they're safe for web, print, and products.

| Role | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Headings and logo | Italiana | Uppercase or title case, slight letter spacing |
| Subheadings, buttons, nav | Jost Light (300) | Uppercase, wide letter spacing |
| Body text | Cormorant Garamond Medium (500) | Use 19px or larger on the web; Medium, not Light, for readability |
| Accent | Mrs Saint Delafield | One or two words only ("xo, Katie"), never sentences |

## Usage rules

- Leave clear space around every logo equal to the height of the "C" in CHAMPAGNE.
- Don't stretch, recolor outside the palette, or add shadows to the logos.
- Use the favicon or monogram anywhere smaller than 120px wide. The full logos need room.
- Use the Champagne Gold only in small amounts. It's the sparkle, not the main color.

## Before this is final
