Quick questions

The stuff you're wondering before you book your blog coaching.

How long is each call? +
Each call is 60 minutes of focused, one-on-one time. Enough to actually dig in and leave with a real plan.
How do I use a multi-call package? +
However works for you. Most women space their calls out over a few weeks or months so they have time to act between sessions. We'll find a rhythm that fits your life.
What if I need more calls later? +
Easy, you can book another package anytime. Lots of women start with a Power Hour and come back for more once they feel the difference.
Are the calls really just me and you? +
100%. This is private 1:1 coaching, no group, no assistant. Just you, me, and your blog.
I'm brand new. Is coaching right for me? +
Totally. The earlier we talk, the more time and mistakes I save you. We'll start exactly where you are, even if that's a blank page.
What's the difference between this and Blog Launch Lab? +
Blog Launch Lab is my full done-with-you program where I also build your website. These packages are pure coaching by the call, perfect when you want guidance and want to do the building yourself.
