Before you join

Fair questions, honest answers

Is this really one-on-one?

Yes. Every weekly call is private, just you and me. Fast Track is the only way to get one-on-one coaching with me.

How is this different from the Blog Build Club?

Same order, same method. Fast Track is faster (8 weeks vs 3 months), private instead of group, and includes written recaps, a monthly done-for-you piece, and a Launch Day Kit.

I'm a total beginner. Can I still launch in 8 weeks?

Yes. Fast Track is built for beginners who want to move quickly with close support.

Is blogging still worth it with AI everywhere?

Yes, the way we build it. Traffic-and-ads blogging is getting harder. A blog that builds trust, grows your email list, and sells your own digital products still works, because AI can't replace you.

My niche already feels crowded. Will I stand out?

That's exactly what our private calls are for. We sharpen who you help, what you sell, and how you say it, so you don't sound like everyone else, human or AI.

Can I use AI to help?

Yes. Use it to brainstorm and draft faster. Your voice and your products are what people buy, so those stay yours.

What's a done-for-you piece?

One thing I build for you each month. We choose it together, based on what your blog needs most right then. Think: a sales page draft, a freebie design, or a welcome email series.

Do I need Showit, a domain, and email marketing?

Yes. You'll buy and keep your own accounts, so everything stays yours.

Do I need professional photos?

Yes. You'll need photos of yourself, delivered on time, so your 8-week launch stays on track.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. Fast Track is built as a 6-month program, so if you leave early, you won't see the full results. You keep everything you built. Payments already made are not refunded.

I have another question. How do I reach you?

Email me at hello@bossladybloggers.com.
