Fair questions

Blog coaching, answered

What does a blog coach do?

A blog coach looks at your specific blog, offer, and situation and tells you what to do next. On my calls you bring the decision you're stuck on, your pricing, your offer, your messaging, and you leave with a clear direction instead of more general advice. I've been coaching bloggers since 2017, with 300+ clients and students.

How much does blog coaching cost?

My coaching starts at $150 for a single one-hour call (The Power Hour). Three calls (The Momentum Pack) are $400, and six calls (The Deep Dive) are $750. Payment plans are available on the packages, just ask.

Is one call really enough?

For one decision, usually yes. For building and launching something, no, and I'd rather tell you that upfront than sell you a single call and watch you drift. If you're in build mode, three is the honest minimum.

What if I don't have a blog yet?

Then coaching probably isn't where to start. Book a call if you want to talk through the idea, but if you're starting from nothing, the Blog Accelerator will get you further faster.

Can I use my calls whenever I want?

Within reason, yes. The three-call pack is good for six months and the six-call pack for nine, so they don't sit unused for two years.

My handle is $Bossladybloggers, and each package has its own secure payment link right on this page: $150 for The Power Hour, $400 for The Momentum Pack, and $750 for The Deep Dive. After you pay, email me your receipt at hello@bossladybloggers.com and we'll schedule your first call. Payment plans are available on the packages, just ask.

Do you offer refunds on packages?

I don't. Once a package is booked it's yours to use. If something comes up in your life, message me and we'll work out the scheduling.
