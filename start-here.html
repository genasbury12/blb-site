the truth Before you write another blog post

There's something I want you to understand. Your blog is not your business.

Your blog is how people discover your business. But the business?

The business is
  • The person you're helping.
  • The problem you're solving.
  • The offer you're selling.
  • And the process that turns interested people into paying customers.

You can have 100 blog posts, a beautiful website, and thousands of monthly visitors... and still have no idea how you're supposed to make money. Because getting traffic and making sales are two completely different things.

That's why I teach blogging differently. I don't want you spending the next two years chasing pageviews without knowing how those pageviews could turn into customers. I want you building a business from the beginning. And I've created several ways to help you do exactly that.
