The two-minute version

The two-minute version of what I believe

if you only read one thing on this site, read this

Your blog isn't your business. It's your marketing. The blog is how people find you and decide they like you. Your offer is how you get paid. Build the blog without the offer and you've built a beautiful machine pointing at nothing.

Ads and affiliates pay pennies, and pennies need crowds. Both need huge traffic before they turn into money you'd notice. Traffic is harder to build now than it's ever been. Keep affiliate links on the side if you like them. Skip ads entirely.

The order matters more than the effort. Decide what you're selling. Write the copy that sells it. Then build the site around that copy. Almost everyone does it backwards, and that's why so many gorgeous blogs make nothing.

I spent two years blogging the normal way and made zero dollars. Then I switched the order and made $4,000 in four months. That's the whole thesis.
