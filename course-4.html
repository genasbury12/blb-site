Creative Tool 01 · interactive

The Reader Card

if it is not for her, it does not get written
saved, babe ✦
My One Readeri write everything to her
her name goes here
e.g. nine posts, a full-time job, 40 subscribers, no offer
one thing — the wall she is standing in front of this week
if she has read forty free posts on this, you cannot be post forty-one
the part she dreads — your offer is usually sitting right here
her phrases, not yours
How to use this cardprint it. tape it up. write to her.

Fill it in here (it saves automatically on this device), then print it and put it somewhere you can see it while you write. When you are three paragraphs into a post and you cannot tell how honest to be or how basic to start, look up at the card and ask her. She is real now. Keep her that way.

The Gatebefore this map counts

The rule that makes the map work: if it does not hang off a branch, it does not get written. yes, even the one you are excited about

  • My lane says what it is and who it is for, not just a topic.
  • I ran the shrink test three times and the last one felt slightly uncomfortable.
  • Every pillar could hold twenty posts, not three.
  • All five branches are filled in. No blanks, no “I will come back to this one.”
  • My review date is on my actual calendar, ninety days out.
leave them up. just stop making more.
date them. nothing comes off this list for ninety days.

narrow is not a cage, babe. it is the door.
